This notice explains data handling for Cuebright, cuebright.com, downloads and Company support. Contact info@backyardbeertaps.com or 3350 NW 124th Way, Sunrise, Florida 33323, United States.

The current App performs transcription, document search and AI generation locally on your Mac. It does not upload your meeting audio, transcripts, documents, questions or answers to us or a cloud AI service as part of those functions. There is no Cuebright account, app usage analytics, advertising SDK or automatic crash-report submission in the current release.

Local processing is different from information you choose to send elsewhere. 2 Information processed locally

The App uses microphone and system audio to transcribe calls you manually start recording. It uses meeting information, notes, documents, derived search data and your questions to provide summaries, answers and action items. Accounts and contacts created inside Cuebright are local organizational records, not online Cuebright accounts. Version 0.4.2 supports one-on-one calls. “You” and “Them” reflect the microphone and call-audio channels, not identification of a person by their voice. Speech detection in this release does not compute speaker embeddings or compare voices across meetings.

Most App content is stored under ~/Library/Application Support/Cuebright. Settings and acceptance records use macOS preferences, and some working files use macOS temporary storage. Content is not encrypted separately by the App. Your macOS account security and FileVault, if enabled, help protect it; they do not prevent every kind of access while your account or device is in use.

The App does not deliberately write call, transcript, document or AI-answer content into its technical system logs. Operating-system facilities, device management, other software, memory handling and backups are outside the App's full control. We do not receive the App's local technical logs automatically.

3 Local retention and deletion

Call audio is stored locally while transcription is pending or running. After transcription completes, the App removes the working audio rather than moving it to Trash, including when a later summary step fails. Interrupted work may remain for recovery when you reopen the App; cleanup of abandoned working files also occurs at launch. If you do not reopen the App, unfinished recordings or temporary files can remain until you remove them. The session-recording folder is marked to be excluded from Time Machine; other backups and copies may behave differently. The App is not an audio archive.

Saved meetings contain transcripts and, when generated, summaries, notes and action items. They remain until you delete them or the relevant App data. A failed summary does not delete the saved transcript.

Imported documents are copied into App storage and indexed locally. Removing an imported document removes its App copy and associated search data, not the original. For connected folders, the App reads source files without writing, renaming, moving or deleting them. It stores extracted text and search data locally.

Removing a source file or disconnecting a folder removes the associated derived data when the change is detected and processed. Cloud-synced folders continue syncing through your provider. Reading an online-only file may cause that provider to download it to your Mac. Changes on network drives may not be detected until the next launch.

Local accounts and contacts remain until deleted. Deleting a contact or account should not be assumed to remove mentions already contained in meeting records or other documents; review those separately. Questions and live answers in Ask are held in memory rather than intentionally saved as a conversation history, unless you separately save or export them. They are discarded when the App exits.

Temporary working files, including setup-test audio, are removed after use, with leftover cleanup on a later launch. Preferences and agreement records may remain until the App's preferences are removed. Merely deleting the application does not remove all these locations. Our data details page explains the relevant storage locations and removal steps.

Deletion inside Cuebright does not delete Time Machine or other backups, exported Markdown files, copied text, originals in connected folders, support emails already sent, or action items sent to Reminders. It is ordinary file deletion, not guaranteed secure overwriting of SSD storage. Review each separate destination if you need to remove those copies.

4 Permissions and integrations

Microphone permission captures your side of a call. macOS screen/system-audio permission enables audio capture; Cuebright does not use it to record screen images. Optional Calendar access reads calendar information locally to show upcoming meetings. Optional Reminders access writes an action item only when you request it. Calendar titles and attendees are read to show upcoming meetings and are not saved into meeting records.

Calendar and Reminders may already synchronize through Apple or another account you configure. Exported content or files in a cloud-synchronized folder may also leave your device under your own configuration. Those services process information under their own terms and settings. We do not receive the synchronized copies through these integrations. You can manage permissions in macOS settings.

Setup offers two optional checks, each started by its own button. The microphone test captures microphone audio to measure volume until you stop it or leave the step; its temporary file is then deleted, with interrupted-test cleanup at the next launch. It does not transcribe the test. The speakers and call-audio check plays a chime and captures only Cuebright’s own chime, not microphone audio or other apps’ sound. Run the microphone test where it will not capture an active call or an unconsenting bystander.
