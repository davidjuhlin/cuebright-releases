2 Local information and storage

The App captures microphone and call audio only when you start recording. It processes transcripts, documents, notes, contacts, questions and generated answers to perform requested functions. Local Accounts organizes companies and people; it is not an online sign-in account. The supported local release uses separate audio channels for “You” and “Them” and speech detection without speaker identification or voice matching.

On macOS, most content is in ~/Library/Application Support/Cuebright; preferences and agreement records use macOS preferences, and working files may use temporary storage. The App does not separately encrypt stored content. Device access controls, FileVault where enabled, and your backup settings affect protection. The App does not deliberately put call, document or answer content in its technical system logs or automatically send those logs to us. Other software, operating-system facilities and backups are outside its full control.

Audio is kept while transcription is pending or running and removed after transcription completes, including if a later summary step fails. Interrupted processing may leave audio until recovery or removal. Saved transcripts, summaries, notes and action items remain until deleted. Temporary files are removed after use, with interrupted-work cleanup on a later launch. Cleanup cannot run while the App stays closed. Deletion is ordinary file removal, not guaranteed secure overwriting.

Imported documents have local copies and search data. Connected folders contribute extracted text and search data; Cuebright does not write, rename or delete source files. Removing an import or disconnecting a folder removes associated App copies or search data. Source-file changes take effect when detected and processed; network-drive changes may wait until next launch. Deleting a contact or company does not necessarily remove references in other records. Questions and live answers are not intentionally retained as a conversation history unless saved or exported. Data Details identifies any release-specific exceptions.

Deleting the App does not necessarily delete its data or preferences. Deleting content in the App does not delete exports, independent backups, copied text, support submissions or synchronized reminders. Pending recording folders are marked for Time Machine exclusion; other backup tools may behave differently. Keep independent copies where needed.

3 Permissions and other services

Cuebright requests microphone permission and the macOS permission needed to capture audio from other apps. The permission’s name depends on the operating system and release. Cuebright does not capture screen images. Optional calendar access shows upcoming event titles and attendees; these are not saved in meeting records. Sending an action item to Reminders is your choice, and your configured provider may synchronize it.

Connected cloud folders continue synchronizing under your own provider’s settings. Reading online-only files may cause that provider to download them. The microphone test temporarily captures audio to measure volume without transcription until stopped or you leave the step; interrupted-test files are cleaned up next launch. The speaker check plays a chime without capture. The call-audio check captures Cuebright’s own chime, not the microphone or other apps’ sound.

5 Optional usage summaries

Only after you choose Share usage summaries, the App collects daily feature-use and reliability counts, approximate duration and response-time buckets, app and operating-system versions, local answer-model tier, document-count bucket, days since opt-in and your role if you choose to provide it. Counts can include calls, imports, submitted questions, completed answers, meetings opened after processing, use of local Accounts, ratings, missing or uncertain answers, selected output actions and failures or crashes. Crash reporting here means counts only, not diagnostic logs, stack traces or memory dumps.

A random installation identifier links participating days so we can understand repeat use, reliability and feature adoption. It is not your name, but the linked records are not described as anonymous. We do not collect call-language codes in this version. No audio, transcripts, document names or contents, notes, questions, answers, calendar content, names, email addresses, file paths or content identifiers are included. The App transmits no keystrokes or click details. A local activity check only determines whether it was actively used that day.

We use these summaries to evaluate and improve Cuebright, not for advertising targeting. We do not link them to billing, support, shared examples or identifiable customer records. Cloudflare hosts the collection service and database. We disable application request logging for this service and do not store IP addresses or precise receipt times in the usage records. Cloudflare still processes network request information to deliver and secure the service.

You can inspect what is shared and turn sharing off in Settings. No usage-summary collection or identifier is created before opt-in and no earlier activity is backfilled. Turning sharing off stops collection and further usage uploads, clears queued summaries and requests deletion of records linked to that installation. If offline, the App retains only the credentials needed to retry deletion; it reports deletion as pending until confirmed. Deletion requests and necessary responses may still use the network. Uninstalling before reconnection can prevent delivery of the request.

Daily installation-level records are removed within 90 days of their activity date. An installation registration is removed after 90 days without an upload; active registrations remain while sharing continues. Unsent summaries expire after seven days. On a confirmed deletion request, linked usage records and registration are removed from the live analytics database. Isolated backup copies expire within the verified period in Data Details, no more than 30 additional days under this design, and are not used for analytics. A restoration process reapplies deletions before normal use resumes.

We may retain statistics without installation identifiers only where they no longer reasonably identify or single out a person. Small groups are combined or excluded before long-term retention; hiding a small number on a dashboard alone does not qualify. Otherwise the 90-day rule applies. Minimal evidence of a privacy choice or deletion may be retained separately where necessary to demonstrate compliance or prevent restoration, without retaining the usage history for that purpose. Contact us for the applicable retention explanation.

We do not send original examples to external generative AI. Synthetic tests must remove identifying and confidential details before any such tool is used. Examples are not linked to the usage-summary identifier.

Only share information you are authorized to disclose. Remove other people’s personal information and confidential, health, financial-account or credential information unless an appropriate reviewed arrangement expressly permits it. Agreement to recording does not by itself authorize sharing a conversation with us. If uncertain, use a synthetic example. Submitting a checkbox does not replace another person’s rights.

We delete original examples within 30 days after resolution and in all cases within 90 days after receipt, unless a narrowly documented legal obligation or preservation need requires otherwise. Earlier deletion can be requested using the submission receipt or by contacting us. Backups expire as specified in Data Details and are excluded from ordinary use. We may retain a genuinely nonidentifying synthetic test. A person mentioned in a submission may also contact us; a receipt is helpful but is not required to exercise applicable rights.
