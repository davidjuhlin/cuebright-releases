2 Local release

Transcription, summaries and document answers use local models unless you separately activate a supported cloud feature. One-on-one labels use microphone and call-audio channels. Speech detection does not identify speakers. Meetings store transcripts and generated summaries, notes and action items. Accounts and contacts are local organizational records. Imported documents have local copies and search data. Connected folders contribute extracted text and search data without changes to the source files.

Audio remains while transcription is pending or running and is removed after transcription completes, including if summary generation fails. An interruption may retain audio until resumed or removed. Saved transcripts remain until deleted. Most content is under ~/Library/Application Support/Cuebright. Pending audio is in session-recordings beneath that folder. macOS preferences and temporary directories contain settings, agreement records and working files. Cleanup of interrupted working files occurs on a later launch. Deletion is ordinary file removal, not secure overwriting.

Use the App to delete meetings, contacts and accounts, remove imports or disconnect folders. Removing one record does not erase references elsewhere. Source changes are reflected when detected and processed; network drives may wait until next launch. Uninstalling does not delete all App data. Backups, exports, source documents and synchronized reminders need separate handling. Content is not separately encrypted by the App; protect the device and consider FileVault.

Cloud folders may download online-only files for reading. Their provider continues synchronization independently. Calendar titles and attendees display upcoming meetings and are not saved in meeting records. Reminders writes are user-directed and may synchronize. Permission names differ by macOS/release; Cuebright captures audio, not screen images. Test Audio is available during setup and later: microphone captures temporarily for volume only, speaker check plays a chime, and call-audio check captures only Cuebright’s own chime. Each is separately started by the user.

3 Downloads and providers

GitHub delivers website, update policy and app updates. Cloudflare delivers installers/models and operates our website download counter. The counter stores only totals by calendar day and installer version, not IP addresses, browser details, referrers, cookies or times of day. These providers necessarily process network information and may keep their own delivery/security records under their policies; we do not receive their individual logs through the counter. Update checks occur at launch and approximately daily after acceptance. Needed models and updates may download in the background. Requests contain no meeting or document content.
